Our Story

Lulwa Alazemi

In this space website project, I worked on two pages, Home page and our story page. The design in the two web pages I worked on are similar with each other and the reason behind choosing this layout, color and design is to modernize our website and make it more appealing for people who would like to visit our page. There are quite a few difficulties and challenges I faced while building the two pages. First, I had a problem with the code of JavaScript and the form. Allowing users to be able to put their information into an appropriate text box was simple enough. However, I had to study more about to get the code working more accurately. The second challenge I had was using the transitioning, because we wanted the website to be more attractive and modern, I added some animation to smooth the contents out. Overall, the project turned out to look very nice my experience with my teammates was pleasant. Also, we learned a lot of useful tricks and ideas from each other while creating this website.

Omar Albalooshi

I was tasked with completing the solar system page. After further research, I found that it was difficult to find information on solar systems as it was a topic that scientists haven’t delved deeply into. I managed to include and answer important questions relating to the solar system and explain the basis of what the solar system is. My first issue was finding the right image for the webpage as images of solar systems on the internet weren’t good enough. After discussing with my teammates, we concluded that a simple image would be good enough as our goal for the website was to make it simple and user friendly. Our goal was to make the website as modern as possible, and to do that we were forced to learn a lot of things that we didn’t previously know. The first thing was flex and beforehand I was only introduced on the topic and had no in-depth knowledge of it. Making this website has given a new outlook on flexbox and why all developers use it in their websites. I learned a lot from my teammates, and we combined our knowledge well to create the space website

Nada Ayyah

My part of the project talks about planets in our solar system. After discussion with my teammates, we decided on a simple structure and design for the page to make it easy for the user to navigate through. After adding the necessary images and texts we found it best to add animations to it to make the page interactive and more dynamic. The most important thing was to create a modern website and as we learned in this course the golden rule is to keep it simple and clear. Considering a topic like planets, it was a little tricky to make sure I wasn't adding too much information and images, so I used minimal colors, a dark background and kept it simple. I found the process of creating this page informative and interesting especially because it includes a lot of learning in different areas intellectually and educationally. Overall, the experience of working as a team was fun and helpful as we learned from each other and helped each other out whenever we faced issues.
