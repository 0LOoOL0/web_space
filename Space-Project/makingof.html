Description

Our idea for the website from the start was a space website. We believe that many students and teachers alike can benefit from the information within the website, with space being a very relevant topic in today’s age, many people can use the information within the website as a reference for their projects or just to learn more about space. Our main goal for the site was to keep it simple and user friendly. In the Home page, we wanted to make visually appealing and easy to navigate through. As you can see in (1) Home, the navigation bar is underneath the title, but after further consideration and discussion, we decided it would be more visually appealing and simple to have it on the right side of the page with the title below it in the center. We kept the navigation bar in the same place for all of the webpages making it easy and simple for the user to navigate through. For the main content of (1) Home, we went for three divs that contain links to the other 3 sub-topics as we found this to be a very modern and easy to navigate way of displaying information. We also added some animation to make the page look more appealing. For the sub-topics, instead of flooding these pages with information we opted to include short, essential, and relevant information that the reader would be interested in learning about. For the form below the main content, we tried to make it look mas neat as possible and visually appealing and validated the form correctly. As you can see in (2) The Solar System, our original idea was to display the information with the question coming first and the answer after it with an image related to the question and answer. We got rid of this idea as we found it to be an old technique and instead went for a more modern look to the webpage. For (3) Planets, we went for a more detailed page but filled with relevant information that the user can use. We went for a simple but clean look to the (2) Planets webpage with Mock-up version of (2) Planets being extremely similar to the final design as we felt confident with the original design. For (3) Black Holes, we changed it to be more visually appealing to the user and provide information about black holes which is a topic that scientists haven’t been able to research more about. (4) Our Story, a very simple design detailing our experiences creating the website and what we learned from it and from each other. In terms of accessibility, we used the “alt” attribute to add text to the images, we opted for a very readable and visually appealing font. We adjusted the size of the headings and sub-headings to make it easier to navigate through. We left a sufficient amount of white space so users can easily navigate through the information. Overall, we are proud of the website we have produced
